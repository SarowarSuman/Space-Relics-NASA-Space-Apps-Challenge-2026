Chapter 1

The First Footprints of Hardware

These artifacts became fixed markers of human exploration. Same idea, two very different graveyards.

Moon Artifacts

Descent stages, experiments, flags and tools remain at landing sites, preserved by vacuum and no weather.

Mars Hardware

Landers, rovers, heat shields and parachutes are spread across Martian plains and craters, slowly buried by dust.
